MeetingCoach vs Fireflies.ai: sales machine vs personal coach

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetingCoach · July 29, 2026

The short version: Fireflies is infrastructure for sales teams — record every call, push it to the CRM, let managers review talk-time and keywords across the org. MeetingCoach is the opposite: a private coach for one person, running entirely on your Mac, $20 once. If you manage a pipeline, look at Fireflies. If you want to get better without your calls living in a company dashboard, that's mine.

Disclosure: I make MeetingCoach. Fireflies is a serious company solving a real problem — it's just usually not your problem, and I'll explain the difference.

What each tool actually does

Fireflies sends its bot into your meetings, records and transcribes them, then does team-scale things with the result: syncs to Salesforce or HubSpot, tracks topics across hundreds of calls, scores reps against talk-time benchmarks, feeds manager dashboards. The buyer is usually a sales leader; the product is visibility into the team's conversations.

MeetingCoach has no bot, no dashboard for your boss, and no cloud. It listens to your live meeting on your Mac and gives you — only you — short cues in a screen-share-safe overlay while there's still time to act on them. Nobody else ever sees your calls, your stats, or your bad habits. The whole point is that improvement is easier when it's private.

Where Fireflies wins

  • You run a sales team. Auto-logging calls to the CRM and seeing deal conversations at scale is genuinely valuable, and MeetingCoach doesn't do any of it.
  • Compliance and record-keeping. Some orgs are required to keep call records. Fireflies is built for that world.
  • Everything-platform coverage. Zoom, Meet, Teams, dial-ins, on any OS.

Where MeetingCoach wins

  • The feedback loop is 60 seconds, not 6 days. Fireflies coaching means a manager reviews your call later, maybe. MeetingCoach nudges you mid-sentence, when you can still change the outcome of this call.
  • Your counterpart doesn't see a recorder join. Nothing chills a candid negotiation like a bot named "Fireflies.ai Notetaker" appearing in the participant list.
  • Privacy by architecture, not by policy. Fireflies protects your data with promises. MeetingCoach protects it by never collecting it — transcription and AI run on your Mac, and it works with WiFi off. There is no server to breach, subpoena, or train on.
  • Cost. One Fireflies Pro seat is $216/year. MeetingCoach is $20 total, and your whole career is on one license.

My honest verdict

This is the easiest verdict of all my comparison pages because the buyers are different people. If you're a sales leader instrumenting a team: Fireflies (or Gong, if budget's no object). I'm not your product.

But if you're the person in the meetings — closing deals, running client calls, interviewing — and you want to get sharper without your practice reps being broadcast to a team dashboard, that's exactly who I built MeetingCoach for. $20, once, private forever, refund within 30 days if it's not for you.
