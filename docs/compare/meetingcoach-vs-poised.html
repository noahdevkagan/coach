Disclosure: I built MeetingCoach. Poised deserves real credit — they proved people want coaching during the call, not a report card after it. This page is about where we split.

What each tool actually does

Poised is an AI communication coach for your calls on Zoom, Meet, Teams and the rest. It gives real-time feedback on how you're speaking — filler words, pacing, hedging, confidence — plus post-meeting reports and trend lines so you can watch yourself improve over weeks. It's polished, it's established, and reviewers consistently say the feedback helps.

MeetingCoach coaches a different layer: not how you sound, but what's happening in the conversation. It applies a coaching rubric to the live discussion and cues you on substance — you've been monologuing for two minutes, that was a range not a commitment, sounds like agreement so close it out. Rubrics are yours to customize: sales calls, 1:1s, interviews, fundraising. And the whole thing — transcription and AI — runs on your Mac. No account, no cloud, works with WiFi off.

Where Poised wins

  • Delivery coaching. If your problem is "um," talking too fast, or sounding unsure, Poised has spent years tuning exactly that, and it's good at it.
  • Long-term trend lines. Watching your filler-word count drop over a month is genuinely motivating. MeetingCoach keeps everything local, so there's no cloud progress graph.
  • Windows support. MeetingCoach is Mac-only.

Where MeetingCoach wins

  • It coaches the meeting, not the voice. You can deliver a flawless, filler-free monologue and still lose the deal because you never made the ask. MeetingCoach watches for the stuff that decides outcomes: talk balance, vague commitments, unclosed agreements, questions you didn't ask.
  • Your rubric, your rules. A sales close, a performance review, and an investor pitch shouldn't be coached the same way. MeetingCoach's rubrics are editable — you decide what it watches for.
  • Nothing leaves your Mac. Nothing. Poised processes your speech in the cloud, with an account attached. MeetingCoach has no server side at all — turn WiFi off and it keeps coaching. If your meetings are confidential, this is the whole decision.
  • $20 once vs a subscription forever. A year of any subscription coach costs more than MeetingCoach costs for life.

My honest verdict

If public-speaking mechanics are your bottleneck — filler words, pace, nerves — Poised is a fine choice and their free tier lets you test that cheaply.

If your meetings sound fine but keep ending wrong — no decision, no commitment, you talked the whole time — that's a substance problem, and it's the one I built MeetingCoach to fix. It's $20, it never phones home, and if it doesn't earn its keep in 30 days, email me and I'll refund you.
